## Google Play reveals new performance requirements for apps and games
**Key Takeaway:** Google Play implements new mandatory performance requirements for apps and games (memory, DEX optimization) from Feb 2027, with non-compliant apps facing reduced visibility. Zero-Tap Sign-In becomes mandatory for eligible apps from Apr 2027.

**Metrics:** 25% coverage across optimization, shrinking, and obfuscation

## Only 2,500 of 190,000 mobile games released in 2025 surpassed 500,000 downloads
**Key Takeaway:** A Moloco report indicates extreme competition: only 2,500 out of 190,000 new mobile games in 2025 exceeded 500,000 downloads. Breakout success relies on paid acquisition, long-term monetization, and later-stage whale conversion, with IAP revenue per install up 126% in the US.

**Metrics:** 190,000 new mobile games, 2,500 games surpassed 500,000 downloads, $1.5 billion in IAP revenue, 20 million+ payers, 21% fall in average time to reach $20M revenue, 126% increase in IAP revenue per install (US, 2021-2025), 250% higher ARPPU for Whale-Driven titles (end of year vs. month one), 25% increase for Volume-Driven titles, 81% of revenue from top 5% payers (Whale-Driven, month 10), 54% of revenue from top 5% payers (Volume-Driven, month 10), 15% of eventual whales made first purchase in launch month, 94% of launch-month UA spend used ROAS objectives (breakout titles), 65% for other titles, 93 distinct creatives (breakout titles), 37 distinct creatives (other titles), 47% of creatives introduced months 7-11 (breakout titles), 27% for others

## ZBD Launches Financial Infrastructure for Real-Money Gaming Payments
**Key Takeaway:** ZBD launches a full embedded financial infrastructure for games, covering money in, through, and out. The platform, backed by $90M, offers embedded accounts, payouts, and rewards, enabling two-way financial relationships and extending to PC/console, aiming to boost engagement and loyalty.

**Metrics:** $90M raised

## Google settles UK app developers’ class action lawsuit for $353m
**Key Takeaway:** Google settles a class-action lawsuit with UK app developers for £260 million ($353.21M) over alleged abuse of its dominant position and unfair commissions. The settlement, awaiting approval, allocates £160M for developers and £100M for legal costs.

**Metrics:** £260 million, $353.21M, £1 billion, $1.3bn, 30% commission, £160m, $217m, £100m, $135m

## Apple reportedly saw US App Store spending fall for the first time in a decade, and Tim Sweeney has thoughts
**Key Takeaway:** US consumer spending on Apple's App Store fell 6% in Q2 2026, the first decline in a decade, largely due to legal battles forcing external payment links. Epic Games' Tim Sweeney blames Apple's '30% junk fees' and App Store ads for 'breaking mobile gaming'.

**Metrics:** 6% fall in US App Store spending (Q2 2026), 9% growth a year earlier, 18% contraction in Apple's US commission revenue, 30% commission

## Data digest: Miniclip, Learnings and Tripledot top July’s US ad revenue chart, Smash Fest stats, fundraising news, more
**Key Takeaway:** Miniclip led July's US ad revenue with $47.8M from a diversified portfolio. The 'Smash Puzzle' genre exploded with 70 new titles, and Ten Square Games reported D2C sales at 24% of bookings. Makers Fund closed a new $250M fund, highlighting continued investment in gaming.

**Metrics:** $47.8M US ad revenue (Miniclip), $6.6M (Arrows Puzzle Escape), 14 titles topped $1M (Miniclip), 96% of Hungry Studio's revenue from Block Blast, 79% of King's revenue from Candy Crush Saga, $42.7M US ad revenue (Learnings), 3.4% growth (Learnings), $40.3M US ad revenue (Tripledot), 53M+ arrow-themed puzzle game installs worldwide, 19.3M installs (Miniclip's Arrow Puzzle), 11.9M installs (Learnings' Amaze GO!), 11.4M installs (Miniclip's Arrows Puzzle Escape), 15M+ installs (Smash Fest), $4M IAP revenue (Smash Fest), 70 new 'smash'-titled games, 9M installs (Royal Smash), $1M revenue (Royal Smash), $250M (Makers Fund IV), $1.5bn total assets under management (Makers Fund), $500M (Fund III, 2022), $5bn acquisition (Dream Games), $134M raised (General Intuition), $3M raised (Surge Games), $25.3M (PLN 93.3m) Q2 bookings (Ten Square Games), 14.6% YOY increase (Ten Square Games), $52M (PLN 191.7m) H1 2026 bookings (Ten Square Games), 13.5% QoQ adjusted EBITDA rise, $5.8M (PLN 21.4m) adjusted EBITDA, 24% D2C sales (Ten Square Games Group, June), 33% D2C in Fishing Clash, $5.8M (€5m) seed funding (Entity)

## Mistplay Accelerates Expansion in Rewarded Advertising with MyChips Acquisition
**Key Takeaway:** Mistplay acquired MyChips, expanding its rewarded advertising footprint globally. This move reinforces the importance of opt-in, performance-driven ad formats for superior engagement, retention, and monetization, integrating MyChips' distribution into Mistplay's LoyaltyPlay network.
