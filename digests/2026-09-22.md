## Berkley Egenes: Navigating regulation in a player-anywhere world
**Key Takeaway:** Global game launches are now staggered compliance rollouts. Developers must adopt "compliance by design" for monetization, treating it as platform capability, not legal overhead. Commerce infrastructure providers are key to managing fragmentation and achieving "player-first" compliant economies, where compliance becomes invisible to players.


## Lilith Games' Clash of Critters: A New Hit Rakes in $45.6M in Four Months
**Key Takeaway:** Lilith Games found a new hit with Clash of Critters, generating $45.6M in four months, with its fourth month being the most lucrative ($19.1M). Strategic regional expansion, particularly into Japan and China, significantly bolstered revenue, demonstrating the power of targeted market rollouts and diversified monetization (including web shops).

## Google Settles UK App Developer Class-Action for £260M
**Key Takeaway:** Google settled a class-action lawsuit with UK app developers for £260M ($350M) over alleged unfair Google Play commissions. While Google admitted no wrongdoing, £160M ($216M) will go to developers, highlighting ongoing pressure on platform fees. A separate consumer claim for £2BN is still proceeding to trial, indicating continued scrutiny on platform dominance and potential impact on consumer costs.

**Metrics:** £260 million, $350m, £160m, $216m, £100m, $134.6m, 30%, £2 billion

## Kakao Games Acquires 39.56% of Me2on for $71.5M to Boost Global Expansion
**Key Takeaway:** Kakao Games is acquiring a 39.56% stake in Me2on for $71.5M, aiming to leverage Me2on's strong global publishing network (80%+ overseas revenue) and diverse portfolio (social casino, casual) to accelerate its own overseas expansion. This strategic M&A move marks Kakao Games' new management direction and emphasizes the importance of global reach in a competitive market.

**Metrics:** 39.56%, ₩98 billion, $71.5 million, $181m, ₩12.6 billion, $9.2m, ₩120.9 billion, $88.2m, 80%, 20%, ₩300 billion, $218.8m, $198m

## Oakever Games Faces Multiple Cloning Accusations and Legal Battles
**Key Takeaway:** Oakever Games, known for hits like Meowdoku, is facing multiple copyright infringement lawsuits and cloning accusations, having already lost one case. Publishers are alerting platform holders (Apple and Google) to these practices, which critics argue stifle innovation. This highlights the industry's struggle with intellectual property enforcement and the balance between inspiration and outright copying, potentially pushing more disputes towards litigation.


## Video Games Europe Cautions Against Broad EU KIDS Act Restrictions
**Key Takeaway:** Video Games Europe broadly supports the EU KIDS Act's goal of protecting children but cautions against "sweeping age assurance measures" across all games. The trade body advocates for PEGI/USK as standard, emphasizes parental responsibility, and seeks public consultation, highlighting the ongoing tension between regulation, platform mandates (like mandatory age verification and restrictions on "addictive" features), and industry self-governance.


## Gaming's Evolving Landscape: Nintendo Sunsets Mario Kart Tour, Indie Hits Go Mobile, Microsoft Expands Handheld
**Key Takeaway:** The industry shows diverse strategies: Nintendo is ending Mario Kart Tour support to focus on its console ecosystem, an indie hit like Sultan's Game is expanding to mobile, and Microsoft is doubling down on handheld and cloud gaming. This reflects a trend towards greater accessibility and flexibility, with publishers adapting to changing player expectations across platforms and devices.
