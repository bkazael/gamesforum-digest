# Gamesforum Digest — 2026-08-14

## Data Digest: Webstores, Pokémon Go Spike, Love and Deepspace Crash
**Key Takeaway:** Webstore revenue is exploding across casino and midcore genres, Pokémon Go's 10th anniversary drove a historic monthly spike, and Love and Deepspace collapsed after a content backlash - underscoring both the upside of DTC channels and the fragility of live-service reputation management.

**Metrics:** Monopoly Go $143m US revenue, $52.6m webstore (up 41% H1), Pokémon Go +230% MoM to $73.7m in July, Love and Deepspace down from $38m to $10m, Pixel Flow $104m H1 hybridcasual puzzle leader, Bandai Namco Digital segment profit down 31%, SciPlay revenue down 9% to $182m, DTC up to 29% of revenue, SEA games market to surpass $7bn by 2030, India games market crosses $1bn

## Cheat Code for LTV Growth: Rewarding Existing Players
**Key Takeaway:** Panelists at Gamesforum Hamburg argue retention is becoming 'the new UA' - studios should build embedded, personalized real-money reward systems for their entire player base rather than renting loyalty via rewarded UA, though rewards must multiply strong core design, not replace it.

**Metrics:** 80% of developers say retention strategies are stale, 50% increased retention budgets, 90% considered real-money reward systems, 39% struggle with personalization, 45% say personalization drives future retention, GAMEE saw 10-15% ad impression drop after removing rewarded leaderboard

## Mobile Gaming's Spend-to-Grow Model Is Evolving
**Key Takeaway:** With UA economics worsening, the industry is exploring shifting budget from acquisition channels toward first-party player rewards, which keep value closer to the game and its most engaged spenders rather than paying it out to ad networks and remarketers.

**Metrics:** $25bn industry UA spend in 2025, Marketing ~25% of revenue for sub-$1bn studios vs 15% software average, Paid install share up 10% YoY, Remarketing spend $31.3bn, up 37% YoY, Day-1 retention ~29%, Day-7 8.7%, Day-30 3.2%, 60% of high-value spenders more likely to spend with redeemable rewards, Acquisition partners per app down from 6 to 5.3

## Apple Proposes 15% US Link-Out Fee Amid Epic Settlement Talks
**Key Takeaway:** Apple has proposed a tiered link-out fee structure (15% standard, 10% partner programs, 5% small business) as part of settlement efforts with Epic, but Epic rejects the fees as far outside Ninth Circuit guidance and has ~60 days to file opposition.

**Metrics:** 15% standard app fee, 10% partner program/subscription renewal fee, 5% Small Business Program fee, Previously 27% contempt-related commission, ~60 day opposition window

## July 2026 Mobile Game Charts: Pokémon Go's Comeback, HoYoverse Surge, Gossip Harbor Record
**Key Takeaway:** Pokémon Go's anniversary and first free Go Fest catapulted it 14 spots to 5th place with $141.5m, while HoYoverse titles and Gossip Harbor posted standout results, though overall industry revenue was down 4% YoY despite a 9% MoM rebound.

**Metrics:** Pokémon Go $141.5m, up 229% MoM, 14-spot jump, Honor of Kings $225.1m top grossing, PUBG Mobile $141.5m, up 59%, Gossip Harbor $142.5m, up 8%, record downloads 9.7m, Industry $6.8bn July revenue, down 4% YoY, up 9% MoM, 4.5bn downloads, down 2% YoY, Tencent $691.8m top publisher, up 29%

## Supercell's Mo Co Underperforming, Reboot Continues
**Key Takeaway:** Despite a May relaunch, Supercell's Mo Co is still failing to scale beyond a niche audience with high engagement but weak growth; Supercell insists closure remains 'unlikely' as it continues iterating, contrasting with the earlier shutdown of Squad Busters.

**Metrics:** $6.7m lifetime revenue from 9m downloads since March 2025, US 26%, Japan 12%, Germany 8%, France 7%, South Korea 5% revenue split, Relaunch went live May 2026
