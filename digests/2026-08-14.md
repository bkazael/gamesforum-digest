# Gamesforum Digest — 2026-08-14

## July 2026 Mobile Game Charts
**Key Takeaway:** Pokémon Go's 10th anniversary and first-ever free Go Fest drove a massive revenue spike, jumping 14 chart positions in one month, while Honor of Kings retained the top revenue spot and Turkish/football titles surged on downloads.

**Metrics:** $141.5m Pokémon Go revenue (+229% M/M), Go rose from 19th to 5th place, Honor of Kings $225.1m, Smash Fest +343% M/M installs, $6.8bn total industry revenue, -4% Y/Y +9% M/M, 4.5bn downloads, -2% Y/Y +4% M/M

## MTG Unveils Playamp Brand
**Key Takeaway:** MTG rebranded its Midcore District into Playamp, consolidating six studios and shared services under one identity, with DTC channels now driving over half of divisional revenue — signaling a strategic push toward owned monetization infrastructure.

**Metrics:** $950.6m 2025 revenue, $239.7m adjusted EBITDA, 78% of MTG total revenue, 51% DTC revenue share Q2 2026, 60+ games supported

## Netmarble Q2 2026 Earnings
**Key Takeaway:** Netmarble posted $505.1m revenue (+4% Y/Y, +15% Q/Q) driven by RPG strength and new launches, with net profit up 27% Y/Y despite operating profit and EBITDA declines, highlighting margin pressure even amid top-line growth.

**Metrics:** $505.1m revenue, $137.4m net profit (+27% Y/Y), Operating profit $54m (-21%), EBITDA $75.5m (-15% Y/Y), RPGs 42% of revenue, North America 39% of revenue

## A Decade of Mobile Gaming Downloads (2015-2025)
**Key Takeaway:** The top downloaded mobile games evolved from endless runners and puzzle games (2015) to social/multiplayer phenomena (2020) to casual/hybrid-casual and UGC platforms like Roblox dominating by 2025, reflecting a broader shift toward ad-monetized, low-commitment, socially-driven gameplay.

**Metrics:** Among Us 424m downloads in 2020, Block Blast! #1 in 2025, Roblox #2 in 2025

## UK Under-16 Social Media Ban Impact on Gaming
**Key Takeaway:** The UK's Spring 2027 under-16 social media ban could push young users toward games as social hubs, creating both an engagement opportunity and new regulatory/safety burdens, while eliminating key youth UA and influencer marketing channels for publishers.

**Metrics:** 9 in 10 parents support ban, 2/3 of young people back restrictions on some platforms
