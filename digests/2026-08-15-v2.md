# מעבר לחנויות: DTC, רגולציה ו-Supercell מנסה שוב | Ben's Weekly Digest

## Mo Co is underperforming and will be rebooted (again) – but Supercell says killing it is “unlikely”
**Key Takeaway:** Supercell's Mo Co continues to underperform despite a recent reboot, struggling with broad player retention beyond a niche audience. This raises concerns about its scalability and Supercell's ability to consistently launch new global hits, especially given its low revenue compared to previously canceled projects.

**Metrics:** $6.7m revenue, 9m downloads, 26% US revenue share, 12% Japan revenue share, 8% Germany revenue share, 7% France revenue share, 5% South Korea revenue share

## Francisco Chilão Rocha: Why Digital Fairness Is Reshaping Mobile Gaming
**Key Takeaway:** Digital fairness, encompassing transparent pricing, monetization, advertising, data handling, and age-appropriate design, is increasingly shaping mobile gaming due to the industry's massive scale and diverse player base. Adopting these regulatory principles can transform compliance into a significant competitive advantage.


## Apple proposes 15% US link-out fee as it seeks settlement talks with Epic Games
**Key Takeaway:** Apple has proposed charging a 15% fee on purchases made through external payment links for standard apps (and 5% for small businesses) in the US, following a court order to allow alternative payment methods. Epic Games has rejected this, claiming the fees are excessive and outside judicial guidance, signaling continued legal battles.

**Metrics:** 15% fee, 5% fee

## Pokémon Go revenue rockets to a four-year high of $141.5m during anniversary month
**Key Takeaway:** Pokémon Go celebrated its 10th anniversary with a four-year high revenue of $141.5 million in July 2026, marking a 229% MoM increase. This success highlights the power of expertly executed live operations, a strong IP, and the potential impact of direct-to-consumer (D2C) channels which contribute significant unrecorded revenue.

**Metrics:** $141.5m revenue, 229% month-over-month increase, 5th global rank, $50.1m US revenue, 35% US share, $38.9m Japan revenue, 27% Japan share, $7.1m South Korea revenue, 5% South Korea share, $90.4m App Store revenue, 64% App Store share, $51.1m Google Play revenue, 800m lifetime players, 100bn km walked, 1tn Pokémon caught, 30% D2C revenue (industry comparison)

## MTG unveils Playamp as new brand for midcore games division
**Key Takeaway:** MTG has rebranded its midcore games division to Playamp, consolidating IAP-driven studios and shared services including marketing, data, AI, and D2C infrastructure. Notably, 51% of Playamp's Q2 2026 revenue came from D2C channels, demonstrating the strategic importance and profitability of direct player engagement.

**Metrics:** $950.6m revenue (2025), $239.7m Adjusted EBITDA (2025), 51% DTC revenue (Q2 2026), 60+ games

## Liftoff posts $220m revenue in first results since IPO
**Key Takeaway:** Liftoff Mobile reported strong Q2 2026 revenue of $220 million, a 35% YoY increase, marking its eleventh consecutive quarter of growth since its IPO. The company attributes its success to its Cortex machine learning platform, highlighting the critical role of AI in navigating the complex UA landscape post-SKAN.

**Metrics:** $220m Q2 revenue, 35% YoY growth, 7% sequential growth, $219m core advertising revenue, 36% YoY ad growth, $132m Adjusted EBITDA, 60% margin, $4m GAAP net loss, $45m non-cash charges, $50m free cash flow, $472.4m IPO raise, $418.4m debt paid, Q3 guidance: $217m-$222m revenue, Q3 guidance: $124m-$128m Adjusted EBITDA, Full-year guidance: $870m-$880m revenue, Full-year guidance: $510m-$518m Adjusted EBITDA, 3.7% share drop, $685.7m 2025 revenue, $23.1m 2025 net loss

## The Rise of Direct-to-Consumer Gaming: Xsolla's Berkley Egenes on DTC Strategies and Web Shops
**Key Takeaway:** The momentum behind D2C strategies is driven by economic benefits (reducing store fees, UA costs), data ownership, and reduced platform dependency. While D2C web shops empower publishers with direct player relationships, they face significant operational and regulatory hurdles, necessitating a regionalized, compliant approach to truly strengthen community and LTV.
