## D2C isn't a payment method: Inside a webstore doing 70% of revenue
**Key Takeaway:** Gamesture increased webstore share from 32% to 70% by treating it as a live product with exclusive content, loyalty programs, and optimized offers, proving D2C is about game economy design, not just payment fees.

**Metrics:** 32% to 70% of sales, 71% of visitors had never seen the main page, 99% clicked through to Games, 10% price discount, 10% value bonus, gifts every $200 spent, gifts every $10 to $50, $250 and $500 top of the ladder, purchase limits raised to five or 10

## August 2026 mobile game charts: Fate/Grand Order’s big anniversary, Valorant Mobile's rise and Brawl Stars’ worst month of the year
**Key Takeaway:** August 2026 charts highlight the power of anniversary events (Fate/Grand Order, Valorant Mobile China) for revenue spikes despite an overall 7% monthly decline in global downloads. Established titles (Subway Surfers, Pokémon Go) leverage nostalgia but require continuous updates, while new launches face intense competition.

**Metrics:** Fate/Grand Order $57.7 million (August), more than doubling July’s $22.5m, 89% of its earnings from Japan, $4.6m on the 15th (daily spending), Valorant Mobile $53.7m (China), up 68% M/M and 375% Y/Y, Tencent’s portfolio grew in revenue and installs, up 9% and 33% respectively, Pokémon Go made $71.7m in August, down 49% M/M, eFootball made $83m in August, down 15%, TCG Pocket made $41.9m, up 49% increase over July, Brawl Stars picked up $24.5m in August, Eggy Party made just $19.6m, a 38% fall, Honor of Kings $218.1m, Gossip Harbor $160.5m (up almost 13%), PUBG Mobile $157m (grew by 11%), Royal Match $155.4m (grew by 10%), Roblox $143.7m (down 1%), Collectively, mobile games generated $6.8 billion (down 6% Y/Y but up 1% M/M), Gangstar Mirage City: Online 12.2m installs, Mystery Dumpling: Unbox ASMR 9m downloads, Block Out 10.4m installs (up 28%), Smash Fest 6m downloads (down from 10.2m), Fortnite picked up just shy of 6m new downloads, Mini Soccer: Football Cup 2026 6.2m new installs (62% rise over July), Royal Smash 6.6m new players (up 36% M/M), Roblox 21.8m new installs (down 5%), Garena Free Fire Max 21.7m installs (down 5%), Subway Surfers 15.3m new installs (22% downloads surge), Block Blast 15.1m downloads, Arrow Puzzle 13.1m, installs totalled 4.2bn (down 7% Y/Y and M/M), Tencent generated $755.9m (9% M/M increase), Century Games $320.3m (rising just 3% M/M), Dream Games $210.9m, Microfun $199.2m, Playrix $190m (decline of just 3% M/M), Scopely combined $243.5m, Miniclip led with 93m new installs (down from 107.5m), Azur Games’ new downloads declined from 74.7m to 72.6m, Voodoo’s fell from 69.7m to 67.1m, Outfit7’s 52.6m installs, BabyBus ranked fifth with 51.9m downloads (growth rate of 1% M/M), Tencent saw stronger growth in downloads, up 33% M/M to 40.2m

## Apple’s new EU App Store terms face backlash from game and app developer groups
**Key Takeaway:** A coalition of 18 developer organizations has strongly criticized Apple's revised EU App Store terms, arguing that the 15% steering fee and 5% Core Technology Commission for external transactions still violate the DMA by maintaining Apple's gatekeeping power and hindering fair competition, despite Apple's claims of collaboration with the EC.

**Metrics:** 18 organisations, 15% fee for steering, 5% Core Technology Commission

## Roblox plans to let creators turn their games into standalone apps
**Key Takeaway:** Roblox is empowering creators with new features like "Roblox Everywhere," allowing them to launch their experiences as standalone apps across mobile, PC, and console, and introducing a Roblox Wallet for daily real-currency payouts, significantly expanding the platform's ecosystem and creator monetization opportunities.

**Metrics:** $5bn through DevEx since 2013, $1.7bn of that earned in the past year alone, close to 9,000 games published through Build, 71% of those creators had never used Roblox Studio before

## Why Nostalgia Is Driving Mobile Gaming’s Biggest Success Stories
**Key Takeaway:** Nostalgia acts as a powerful acquisition tool in mobile gaming by leveraging familiar IPs and emotional connections. However, long-term success, as demonstrated by games like Subway Surfers, depends on continuous evolution and adaptation of content to keep the experience fresh for both veteran and new players.

**Metrics:** Subway Surfers sat at number one (2015), remained in the top three in 2020, still number four in 2025

## Sensor Tower Acquires AppMagic to Expand SMB Gaming Intelligence Offering
**Key Takeaway:** Sensor Tower's acquisition of AppMagic expands its market intelligence reach to the SMB segment, offering more accessible competitive data and trend analysis to independent developers and smaller studios, thereby consolidating its position as a comprehensive analytics provider across the entire mobile gaming ecosystem.

**Metrics:** AppMagic founded in 2016, 149 billion downloads globally in 2025
