# Gamesforum Digest — 2026-08-13

## Mobile Gaming’s Spend-to-Grow Model Is Evolving
**Key Takeaway:** UA spending is hitting diminishing returns as paid install share rises and retention collapses within a month, pushing studios to redirect budget toward first-party, real-money rewards for existing players instead of only chasing new installs.

**Metrics:** $25 billion UA spend in 2025 (AppsFlyer), marketing ~25% of revenue for studios under $1B vs 15% average software (Bain), paid install share +10% YoY on Android and iOS in 2025, remarketing spend $31.3B in 2025, up 37% YoY, Day-1 retention ~29%, Day-7 ~8.7%, Day-30 ~3.2%, ~60% of high-value spenders and ~50% of overall spenders more likely to spend with redeemable rewards (Mistplay), average acquisition partners per app down from 6 to 5.3

## Mobile games make $6.6bn in July as Honor of Kings leads player spending
**Key Takeaway:** Global mobile game spend continues to grow, with Honor of Kings topping revenue and Roblox leading downloads; web shop monetization is increasingly significant, especially for casino and midcore titles like Monopoly Go and miHoYo.

**Metrics:** $6.6 billion global spend in July, up ~8% MoM, 3.7bn global downloads, up 2% MoM, Monopoly Go: $52.6m US webstore in June + $90.1m app store IAP; webstore revenue +41% H1 2026, store IAP -25%, miHoYo webstore share up from 7% (H1 2024) to 57% (H1 2026) of US purchase revenue, 6 of top 10 US webstore mobile games are casino genre

## Tripledot acquires Supersonic from Unity for $40m
**Key Takeaway:** Tripledot continues its aggressive consolidation strategy, following its $800m AppLovin games acquisition with a $40m purchase of Supersonic from Unity, strengthening its hyper-casual publishing and scaling capabilities.

**Metrics:** $40m acquisition price, Supersonic surpassed 6.2 billion lifetime downloads, Prior Tripledot/AppLovin deal: $800m

## Cheat Code for LTV Growth: Why Rewarding Existing Players May Be Mobile Gaming’s Next Growth Lever
**Key Takeaway:** Industry experts at Gamesforum argue retention is becoming 'the new UA,' with embedded real-money rewards for existing players seen as a product-led growth lever rather than just a marketing tactic — but rewards only amplify good game design, they don't fix weak products.

**Metrics:** 80% of developers say retention strategies are becoming stale (ZBD study, 195 leaders), 50% of studios increased retention/re-engagement budgets in past year, 90% have considered in-game real-money reward systems, 39% struggle to tailor experiences to user segments, 45% believe personalization will drive future retention gains, GAMEE saw 10-15% drop in ad impressions after removing a rewarded leaderboard feature

## Pokémon Go revenue rockets to a four-year high of $141.5m during anniversary month
**Key Takeaway:** Pokémon Go's 10th anniversary and free Go Fest 2026 event drove its best month in four years, showing how live events and D2C/webstore strategies can reignite spending in a legacy title.

**Metrics:** $141.5 million July revenue, up 229% MoM, US $50.1m (35%), Japan $38.9m (27%), South Korea $7.1m (5%), App Store $90.4m (64%), Google Play $51.1m, Over $5m single-day spend on July 11, 800m lifetime players, 100 billion km walked, 1 trillion Pokémon caught, Some studios reporting ~30% of revenue from D2C
