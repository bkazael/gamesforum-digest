## Discussion questions (editorial prep)
- **How are the significant year-over-year revenue declines in global mobile game IAP (Article 3) and US publisher revenue (Article 4) being offset by growth in markets like Türkiye (Article 4)?**
  - [ARTICLE] While global mobile game IAP fell 7% year-over-year to $19.6 billion in Q3 2026, and US-based publishers saw a 13% drop to $10.2bn in gross IAP revenue over the last 12 months, Türkiye grew 17% to $4.6bn in the same period. This suggests that growth in emerging markets is partially compensating for declines in more established ones.
- **Given Google Play's new AI-driven 'Gamer DNA' discovery engine (Article 2), how should developers prioritize game mechanics and player sentiment in ASO and game design compared to chart performance?**
  - [ARTICLE] Google Play's 'Gamer DNA' and AI-powered discovery engine may shift game discovery from chart dominance to player fit. This implies that ASO and game design should become more focused on mechanics, player sentiment, and specific user preferences, rather than solely aiming for top-chart positions.
- **What is the primary focus and unique selling proposition of Miniclip's new ad network, AdSurge (Article 5), compared to other ad networks?**
  - [ARTICLE] AdSurge, built by Miniclip, focuses on delivering results for partners, measuring performance by ROAS, net ad monetization yield, player experience, and SDK technical performance, rather than prioritizing network yield. It aims to help publishers reclaim value lost to platform fees and other ad network margins.
- **How does the 'Torture Sort' genre (Article 1) specifically leverage player frustration and ad monetization to achieve high revenue, and what are the implications for player retention?**
  - [ARTICLE] The 'Torture Sort' genre, exemplified by JamBus SandFall, uses deliberately slow gameplay (e.g., 17 minutes per level with no speed-up) and an 'ad-gated' mechanic where watching another ad provides more space, creating an endless revenue loop. This strategy generates high revenue through ads, but the article implies it comes at the cost of player retention, with one author noting a 20-25% retention rate.
- **Considering the rise of AI in game development and discovery (Articles 2 & 4), what is the potential long-term cost implication for studios?**
  - [PREVIOUS_EPISODE] Developers need to be aware of the long-term costs of third-party AI services, which could be up to 20 times higher than current operational costs.
- **How can publishers leverage Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) strategies to navigate global commerce complexities and reach players beyond app stores?**
  - [ARTICLE] Publishers can grow D2C revenue by integrating web stores, link-outs, and distribution through local marketplaces and commerce partners. This approach requires a flexible strategy that adapts to local payment preferences, pricing, regulations, and tax differences, providing a locally relevant buying experience to reach new audiences.
