# Notes on recent episodes

Write two or three lines after you listen: what you liked, what dragged, what you want more or
less of. The last three entries are given to the story writers as the listener's taste (never as
facts). To add an entry, start a new line with `## ` and the date, then your notes. For example:

    ## 2026-11-16
    Too fast at the start. More numbers about real-money skill games. Fewer layoff stories in a row.

Edit or delete entries any time; nothing depends on them.
